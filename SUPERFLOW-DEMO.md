TITLE: "Superflow" — 75-second launch film. Design-first, cinematic, restrained.

FORMAT
16:9, 4K, 60-120fps fps, 2.39:1 letterbox on beauty shots, full 16:9 on UI shots. Shot on a locked-off or slow-dolly cinema camera, 50mm and 100mm macro lenses, razor-shallow depth of field on macro shots, deep focus on UI shots. Zero handheld shake. Every move is a slow, deliberate push-in or lateral slide. Real dark-room photography: a MacBook Pro on a matte black desk, one soft cool key light from camera left, nothing else lit. Black stays black.

VISUAL LAW (non-negotiable, this is the brand)
Paper, ink, one accent. Palette only: canvas #0A0A0A, surface #171717, control #1F1F1F, text #FAFAFA, secondary text #A3A3A3, accent blue #2563EB. Blue ramp for data: #172554, #1E3A8A, #2563EB, #60A5FA.
NO glow, NO bloom, NO lens flares, NO gradients, NO drop shadows, NO outlines or strokes on any UI, NO neon, NO holograms, NO sci-fi HUDs, NO floating UI panels in 3D space. Depth comes only from flat luminance steps. UI is always shown flat on the real laptop screen or full-frame, never tilted into fantasy space.
Signature element: the PIXEL. Square cells about 4 px with 2 px gaps, hard-edged, aliasing OFF, snapped to a grid. Pixels appear in whole discrete steps (a 14-step reveal), never smooth fades or tweens. Motion on UI is smooth ease-out, never bouncy, never overshoot, never looping except one 3-cell wait mark.
Type: UI in SF Pro, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS. Titles in a refined italic display serif, always ending in a small blue pixel-art full stop (a 5x5 dithered pixel disc) sitting on the baseline like a period.

THE SPINE
One continuous idea: a single blue pixel becomes a voice, becomes text, becomes a meeting, becomes ten unbroken hours, and returns to a single pixel as the full stop of the logo.

SHOT LIST (cuts land on the downbeat of the score)

0:00–0:05 — BLACK. Total silence for one beat, then a sustained low cello and double bass pedal tone fades up from nothing. Extreme macro of the MacBook's notch, black on black. One 2-pixel blue cell appears on the shelf under the notch. Hold. Nothing else moves.

0:05–0:12 — MACRO, fn KEY. Top-down, then low-angle on the fn keycap. A finger presses and holds. The keycap sinks 4 pt, a soft haptic tick is heard. From under the key, a blue pixel bloom grows across the desk-dark surface in 14 whole dithered steps, in 8 pt cells, hollowed out in a clean organic gap around the on-screen text. Timpani roll begins under it. Release: the bloom collapses back into the key in one clean motion. A line of text types itself in white from a caret: "Speak. It types."

0:12–0:22 — THE ISLAND. Push in on the notch. It widens flush from the hardware into a black notch-shaped silhouette with concave top fillets, opaque black, no glass. Left wing: the target app icon. Right wing: an 11x7 pixel meter, 2 px cells, only lit cells drawn, a symmetric blue envelope radiating from the center, peaks in #60A5FA, silence as a dotted midline. A real sentence appears word by word in a document on screen, perfectly aligned. Release: three 2 px cells step in place (the wait mark), then a single checkmark. Staccato strings enter, tight 16th-note ostinato, in sync with the meter.

0:22–0:30 — LOCAL. Cut to the menu bar: Wi-Fi switches off, ethernet unplugged, airplane mode on. The Island meter keeps moving. The sentence keeps typing. On-screen, one line in white: "Everything stays on this Mac." Held cleanly for 2 seconds. The strings drop out, leaving cello and timpani.

0:30–0:40 — CLEANUP, THE PROOF. Full-frame History window, flat on #0A0A0A. Detail pane shows a "Raw" / "Cleaned" segmented control; a sliding capsule thumb toggles. In Raw, filler words and bad punctuation. Toggle to Cleaned: the changed spans are marked by soft blue wash highlights and the text resolves into clean prose. Then the audio card: an 8-row PIXEL SPECTROGRAM of the recording, columns of square cells, low band at the bottom, a scan column sweeping left to right; unplayed cells are dim neutral, passed cells ignite blue behind a short checkerboard-dithered cooling trail. A cursor drags across it; the picture scrubs. On-screen: "Quality cleanup. Local."

0:40–0:50 — MEETING INTELLIGENCE. Full-frame Meetings view. A 96-cell "speaker barcode" under the meeting title: your cells in blue, remote speakers in three steps of gray, silence in dark neutral. The detail pane: a two-column transcript, speaker names and timestamps pinned in a left column, clean prose on the right. A speaker name is renamed inline. Then a button press: "Copy as Markdown." The clipboard content pastes into a code editor, perfectly structured. On-screen: "Meeting intelligence. Every speaker. Every turn." Brass enters, one slow rising swell.

0:50–1:02 — RELIABILITY. Time-lapse. A tabular counter rolls in large SF Pro Medium numerals: 1h, 2h, 5h, 10h, with a flat blue pixel-line memory graph that stays dead flat. At the peak, the app is force-killed: the window vanishes, a hard black frame, one beat of silence (orchestra cuts to nothing). Relaunch. A quiet card: "Recovered." with the full text restored. Orchestra slams back in, full strings, brass, timpani, cymbal swell. On-screen: "Ten hours in. Nothing lost."

1:02–1:08 — THE LEDGER. Dashboard. Four metrics in one card (Words, Time saved, Average speed, Day streak), numbers rolling. Below, the 12-month graph reveals right to left in 14 stepped columns, twelve small month-calendars of blue cells, today as one white cell like a caret.

1:08–1:15 — RESOLVE. Hard cut to black. In the center, in the italic serif: "Superflow" and the blue pixel disc lands on the baseline as the full stop, travelling along a short sine arc into place. Below, one line: "Local. Private. Unbreakable." (or the user's final tagline). Orchestra resolves to a single sustained chord, then one final timpani hit, then silence. The blue pixel remains for one extra beat, then black.

SOUND DESIGN
No song. No vocals. No pop beat, no synths, no electronic drums, no trailer "braaams," no sound effects except two: the soft keycap tick and the trackpad haptic tick. Pure classical-cinematic orchestration, recorded-in-a-hall feel, wide natural reverb, dry and intimate in quiet moments.
Instrumentation: solo cello and double bass pedal, tight staccato violin and viola ostinato, timpani rolls, French horns and trombones for the swell, snare-less march pulse in pizzicato, harp for the pixel steps, a single cymbal swell at the recovery moment.
Key and tempo: D minor, 4/4, starting at 72 BPM, accelerating to 108 BPM by the reliability shot, resolving to D major on the last chord. Every visual cut and every pixel reveal lands on a beat. The one total silence moment is the crash.

CAMERA AND EDIT RULES
Cuts on the beat only. No whip pans, no dutch angles, no speed ramps, no crossfades (hard cuts and the pixel reveals are the transitions). UI shots are pin-sharp, pixel-aligned, no moire, no blur, no ghosting. Text is perfectly legible, perfectly spelled, never morphing.

glow, bloom, lens flare, gradient backgrounds, neon, purple, teal, holograms, 3D floating UI, tilted UI, drop shadows, borders on cards, rounded outlines, stock footage, people's faces, smiling workers, handwritten text, garbled or morphing text, misspelled words, extra logos, watermarks, bouncy animations, spring overshoot, loops, shaky cam, fish-eye, film grain overload, vocals, songs, electronic music, trap beats.
