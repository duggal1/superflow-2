# Superflow architecture

Superflow records and streams audio locally. `AudioCppRecognizer` provides live ASR. `SessionEngine` owns dictation and streams completed text into cleanup. For dictation, the cleanup pipeline can begin processing sentences while ASR keeps streaming. The engine reuses a cleaned prefix only when its raw words match the final recognizer segment. The remaining tail goes through the normal cleanup queue. Confirmed checkpoints are compacted into one reusable prefix so long sessions keep offering cleanup work; corrections at the live tail and fallback results withhold their text for stop-time cleanup. Domain contracts are in Kernel and Ports. See [the engine plan](plan.md) and [module dependencies](docs/architecture/module-dependencies.mmd).

At stop, the engine waits only a short insertion budget for cleanup and pastes the best text available; unfinished segments use their durable raw ASR words. The coordinator then drains the remaining cleanup before the session is marked complete, so a slow S1-mini pass cannot delay the paste or leave a segment unfinished.

The app builds the text pipeline with `CleanupService` and `LlamaCleaner`. In production the cleaner reaches llama.cpp through the InferenceHelper XPC service. Cleanup uses the pinned S1-mini model, protects exact literals and numbers, validates model output, and reports why it falls back. The app keeps the original text when a guarded model attempt cannot be used.
